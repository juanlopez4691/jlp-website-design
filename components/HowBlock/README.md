Step animation (Penpot board `spec / how step animation`). Intro, once, when the steps scroll into view: every number rises out of its rule (650 ms, `cubic-bezier(0.3, 0.7, 0.2, 1)`, starting 200 ms into the step's reveal, plus 80 ms per column). After the first number lands, 500 ms pause, then the steps play one after the other from left to right: the arrow draws along the rule from the left (700 ms, ease-in-out; transforms only, the head arrives last) while the step lifts 16 px (up by 35%, held to 65%, back by 100%). Each step waits for the previous one and for itself to be in view; steps already scrolled past show drawn, without animation. Hover (mouse or pen, not touch), after the intro: the step replays (arrow redraws, lift) and the number dips into the rule over the first 30% and rises back; a replay always runs to its end; on leave only the lift eases back (300 ms ease-out). With `prefers-reduced-motion` everything is at rest (numbers up, arrows drawn), no intro or hover. Without JavaScript: numbers over a plain 2px rule, no arrow.
