Project card on a teal band. Three parts, top to bottom: head (client name in the `tag` style, uppercase, no pill, 4px gap, then `h3-card`), body (`body-sm`, then one `result` line, 8px gap) and chips. Parts are 16px apart. The body fills the free height, so the chips always sit at the bottom of the card. Cards in one row take the height of the tallest card. Mint fill, `radius-sm`, padding 24.

The client name is plain small caps text. Chips stay as pills.

The **on dark** variant ("More work") is transparent with a 1.5px mint border and mint text. It has no result line.
