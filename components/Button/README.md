Square buttons (`radius-none`, 0) with a 2px border. Padding 13px 24px, `button` text style.

Penpot variants (`Type`): `primary`, `secondary`, `accent` and `secondary-on-dark`.

- **Primary**: `navy` fill, `mint` text. One per block.
- **Secondary**: transparent, navy border and text.
- **On dark blocks** (teal, navy): primary becomes `saffron` fill with navy text, secondary becomes `secondary-on-dark`, with mint border and text.
- Hover swaps fill and outline. Labels in sentence case: "Tell me about your project", "Email me".
