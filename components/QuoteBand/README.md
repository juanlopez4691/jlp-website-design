Full-width coral band (`surface.quote`) that holds one short quote taken from a recommendation. Made of: the section arrow (mint, centred on the top edge), two decorative quotation marks behind the text, and a container (1152px) with the text column, centred. The marks are the SVG `assets/icons/quote-mark.svg` (the “ glyph of Playfair Display SC Bold drawn as a path, so the font is not loaded): an opening mark at the top left and the same mark rotated 180 degrees at the bottom right, in `text.on-dark` at 30% opacity (`opacity-30`). Desktop: 288 wide (`w-72`), 40px from the side (`left-10`, `right-10`) and 16px past the edge (`-top-4`, `-bottom-4`), so the band crops them. Tablet: 192 wide (`w-48`), `left-8`/`right-8`, `-top-3`/`-bottom-3`. Phone: 128 wide (`w-32`), `left-4`/`right-4`, `-top-2`/`-bottom-2`. In Astro they are two absolutely positioned inline SVGs with `aria-hidden="true"` and `pointer-events-none`, inside a `relative overflow-hidden` band, with `fill="currentColor"`. In Penpot they are two absolute children of the band with left/top and right/bottom constraints, sent behind the container. The text column has the quote in `quote-big` (max 896px wide, `text.on-light`) and, 16px below, the author line: name in `lead`, then the role in `body-sm`, 8px apart. The whole block is centred. Padding 80 top and bottom.

Content rules: the quote is a sentence or two from a real recommendation, in the person's own words, without ellipsis tricks. The author is always named. To reuse the band, change the quote, name and role, and override the arrow colour to the colour of the band above.

In Penpot the main component `quote-band` lives on the Components page and contains an instance of `arrow`.
