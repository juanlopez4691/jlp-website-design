A recommendation, content only: no fill, radius or padding (the navy and white boxes belong to `recommendations-block`). The quote comes first, the author underneath, pinned to the bottom of the box: 50px round photo (`size.avatar`, `radius-full`), the name in `lead` at weight 600 followed by the `icon-external-link` (the name links to the person's LinkedIn profile), and the role in `body`, 8px between photo and text. The quote text fills the free height, and cards in one row share the height of the tallest. Long quotes are shortened with a "Read more" toggle; the text is always the person's own words.

Variants: `Style` featured (`quote-big`, Figtree 300, mint, for the navy box) or regular (`lead`, navy, for the white boxes), and `Breakpoint` xl, md, base. On md and base the photo is hidden, and the featured text steps down from 36 to 30 and 24. Penpot name `quote`. In Astro: `Quote` with a `featured` prop, inside a `rounded-[28px]` box (`bg-navy p-12` or `bg-white p-8`) in the block.
