Saffron block (`surface.hero`) at the top of every page, 32px padding top and bottom at `xl`. Below `lg` Penpot has 22px padding around the 44px `menu-button`, which makes the block 88px tall as well; in Astro the padding stays 32px at every size and the button takes a -10px margin on every side (`size-11 -m-2.5`), so its touch area reaches into the padding and the bars stay where they were. From `lg` (1024px): the eight `nav-link`s on the left (32px gaps: Home, Services, Work, Recommendations, Process, Experience, Stack, Contact), `nav-lang` on the right, in the 960 container at lg (the `xl` and `lg` variants are the same layout). Below `lg` (tablet and phone): `nav-lang` on the left and `menu-button` on the right, no links (the `md` and `base` variants). `nav-lang` reads "ES / EN" (Spanish first) with the current language underlined (variant `Lang` en or es). All navigation text, `nav-link` and `nav-lang`, uses the `nav` style (16px, weight 600). The open menu is a separate frame, not part of the component. Penpot name `header`, variants `Breakpoint` xl, lg, md, base and `State` default, pinned.

On scroll, in browsers with CSS scroll-driven animations, the header is sticky. Over the first 32px of scroll it shrinks to 16px padding top and bottom, so the pinned header is 56px tall plus its border. From 1px of scroll it gets a 1px `ink` bottom border, a soft `ink` shadow at 15% (the `shadow-md` shape: `0 4px 6px -1px` and `0 2px 4px -2px`) and a 4px `ink` progress bar on the bottom edge, which grows from left to right as the page scrolls. The bar is decorative. At the top of the page, or on a page too short to scroll, there is no border, shadow or bar. The top row of the open menu shrinks in the same way, so its controls do not move when it opens. In browsers without scroll-driven animations none of this applies: the header is not sticky and has no border, shadow or bar. The `pinned` variants are a static illustration of this, with the bar part-way across. Like the 22px at rest, Penpot gives the `md` and `base` pinned variants 6px padding around the 44px `menu-button`, so they are 56px tall as well.
